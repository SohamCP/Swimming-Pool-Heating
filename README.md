# Swimming-Pool-Heating

Design and techno-economic analysis of a flat-plate collector (FPC) system that keeps a 25 m × 50 m outdoor swimming pool at 29 °C, using a full year (2019) of hourly NSRDB weather data for IIT Roorkee, India.

The study runs in four stages:

1. **Solar radiation**: solar geometry and irradiance on a tilted collector using the isotropic **Liu-Jordan** model and the anisotropic **HDKR** model.
2. **Pool heat load**: hourly losses by evaporation, convection, long-wave radiation and make-up water, with and without a night-time PVC cover.
3. **Component sizing and capital cost**: LMTD sizing of the heat exchanger plus the cost of collectors, cover and storage tanks.
4. **Life-cycle economics**: discounted cumulative savings against a conventional heater, including a loan, fuel escalation and maintenance.
